About Paperbag Hobbies

Precision Crafting for Tabletop Wargaming

Based in Banbury, United Kingdom, Paperbag Hobbies is an independent workshop dedicated to producing high-detail resin miniatures, modular terrain sets, and bespoke tabletop gaming accessories. Founded out of a lifelong passion for sci-fi and fantasy wargaming, our mission is to provide hobbyists with crisp, clean prints that elevate your tabletop battlefields.

Our Production Standards

Every single model and terrain piece we offer goes through rigorous cleaning, UV-curing, and manual support removal inspection. We utilize high-grade engineering resins and durable PLA plastics to ensure your miniatures withstand transport, painting, and the rigors of weekly skirmishes.

Custom Orders & Community

Looking for something specific or customized for your narrative campaign? We regularly handle custom print queue requests. Reach out to our workshop team via email or visit our Shop to explore our current catalog of ready-to-prime miniatures and terrain.

Shipping & Dispatch

Standard dispatch within 3–5 working days. Securely packaged in protective bubble wrap.
